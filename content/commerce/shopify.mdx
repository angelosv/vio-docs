# Connect Shopify to Vio

![Vio Sync for Shopify — connect your Shopify store and make your products shoppable across Vio's network, with orders flowing back automatically.](/commerce/shopify/banner.png)

Sell your Shopify catalogue through Vio in a few minutes — no code required. This guide covers
installing the **Vio Sync** app, connecting your store, syncing your products, and receiving
orders back.

<Callout type="info">
**Who this is for** — Shopify merchants who want their products available in Vio. You'll do
everything from your Shopify admin and the Vio dashboard.
</Callout>

## Before you start

Make sure you have:

- A **Shopify store** on any plan.
- A **Vio API key** — sign up at your Vio dashboard to get one.
- At least one **product** in your store (you need products to sync).

## How it works

Once you connect your store, Vio keeps your catalogue in sync and delivers your orders back:

1. You choose which products to export — titles, prices, stock, variants and images sync to Vio.
2. Your products become shoppable inside Vio's network of publisher apps and articles.
3. When a shopper places an order, it's delivered straight to your Shopify store.

## Step 2 · Get your Vio API key

Find or create your API key from your Vio dashboard — click **Open Vio dashboard** from the
app's connect screen, or go there directly.

<Callout type="warning">
Keep your API key private — it links your store to your Vio account. Treat it like a password.
</Callout>

## Step 3 · Connect your store

Vio Sync opens on **Connect to Vio**. Paste your **Vio API key** and click **Connect**.

<Callout type="info" emoji="🖼️">
**Screenshot** · `commerce/shopify/03-connect.png`
<br />*Shows:* the "Welcome to Vio Sync" connect screen, with the **Vio API key** field, the
**Connect** button, and the "Find your API key in your Vio dashboard" link.
</Callout>

Once connected, a confirmation dialog appears and you land on **Products**.

![The "You're connected to Vio" confirmation dialog after connecting a store.](/commerce/shopify/04-connected.png)

## Step 4 · Sync your products

On the **Products** screen, select the products you want to sell on Vio and click
**Export selected**. Exports run through a queue — each product's badge flips to **✓ Exported**
within a few seconds, without reloading the page.

![The Products table with search, status and export filters, a "Select all" checkbox, and rows showing type badges (Single / N variants) and export status.](/commerce/shopify/05-products.png)

<Callout type="info" emoji="🎥">

<br />*Shows:* selecting products, clicking **Export selected**, and badges turning to
**✓ Exported**. ~20–30 s.
</Callout>

**What syncs:** title, price, stock, variants and images. Only the products you explicitly
select are exported — nothing syncs automatically on install.

## Step 5 · Receiving orders

Orders placed through Vio are delivered to your Shopify store automatically — they appear in
**Shopify → Orders** like any other order, so inventory and fulfillment stay in your normal
workflow. There's no extra setup: the app registers the order webhooks for you during
connection.

## Managing your catalogue

From **Products**, use the search field, the **Status** filter (All statuses / Active / Draft
/ Archived) and the export filter (All products / Exported to Vio / Not exported) to find what
you need, then select products and:

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Export selected** | Push the selected products to Vio. |
| **Remove selected** | Remove the selected products from Vio (with a confirmation step). |

Open **Connection & log** at any time to see your connection status (store, Vio account,
API reachability, products exported) and the list of everything currently exported.

![The Connection & log screen — connection status (store, Vio account "Connected", Vio API "Reachable", products exported) and the export log.](/commerce/shopify/06-connection-log.png)

## Disconnecting

To disconnect, open **Connection & log** and click **Disconnect from Vio**. This removes the
connection between your store and Vio — your products stop being sold on Vio.

## Troubleshooting

<Callout type="warning">
**Can't connect / key is rejected** — double-check the API key you pasted matches your Vio
dashboard exactly, with no extra spaces.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning">
**Vio API shows "Unreachable"** — open **Connection & log** and click **Re-check**. If it stays
unreachable, try again in a few minutes.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning">
**Orders don't arrive** — orders only sync back for products that were exported through Vio
Sync. Confirm the product shows **✓ Exported** on the Products screen.
</Callout>
