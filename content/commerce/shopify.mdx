# Connect Shopify to Vio

<Callout type="info" emoji="🖼️">
**Banner** · `commerce/shopify/banner.png`
<br />*Shows:* the Vio Sync for Shopify hero banner.
</Callout>

Sell your Shopify catalogue through Vio in a few minutes — no code required. Vio Sync is a
**sales channel**: it plugs into Shopify the same way Online Store or POS do. This guide covers
installing the app, connecting your store, publishing your products, and receiving orders back.

<Callout type="info">
**Who this is for** — Shopify merchants who want their products available in Vio. You'll do
everything from your Shopify admin and the Vio dashboard.
</Callout>

## Before you start

Make sure you have:

- A **Shopify store** on any plan.
- A **Vio API key** — sign up at your Vio dashboard to get one.
- At least one **product** in your store (you need products to publish).

## How it works

Once you connect your store, Vio keeps your catalogue in sync and delivers your orders back:

1. You choose which products to publish to Vio — titles, prices, stock, variants and images
   stay in sync. **Nothing is published automatically when you connect.**
2. Your products become shoppable inside Vio's network of publisher apps and articles.
3. When a shopper places an order, it's delivered straight to your Shopify store.

## Step 1 · Install the app

<Steps>

### Find the app

In the Shopify App Store, search for **Vio Sync** and open the listing.

Click **Install**. Shopify shows the permissions Vio Sync is requesting — review them and

## Step 2 · Get your Vio API key

Find or create your API key from your Vio dashboard — click **Open Vio dashboard** from the
app's connect screen, or go there directly.

<Callout type="warning">
Keep your API key private — it links your store to your Vio account. Treat it like a password.
</Callout>

## Step 3 · Connect your store

Vio Sync opens on **Connect to Vio**. Paste your **Vio API key** and click **Connect**.

## Step 4 · Publish your products

You can publish from the app, or from any product's page — both work, use whichever you prefer.

### From the app

Open **Products** in the app navigation. Search or filter your catalogue, select the products
you want to sell on Vio and click **Publish selected**. The **Vio status** column flips to
**Published** and the products sync to Vio right away.

### From a product's page

Vio also shows up in the **Publishing** card on every product page in your Shopify admin —
just like Online Store. Add the product to the **Vio** channel there (or use the bulk editor)
and it syncs the same way.

**What syncs:** title, price, stock, variants and images. Only the products you explicitly
publish go to Vio — nothing syncs automatically on install or connect.

## Step 5 · Receiving orders

Orders placed through Vio are delivered to your Shopify store automatically — they appear in
**Shopify → Orders** like any other order, so inventory and fulfillment stay in your normal
workflow. There's no extra setup: the app registers the order webhooks for you during
connection.

## Managing your catalogue

From **Products**, use the search field, the **Status** filter (All statuses / Active / Draft
/ Archived) and the **Vio status** filter (All products / Published to Vio / Not published) to
find what you need, then select products and:

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Publish selected** | Make the selected products available on Vio. |
| **Unpublish selected** | Remove the selected products from Vio (with a confirmation step). |

Open **Connection & log** at any time to see your connection status (store, Vio account,
API reachability, products published) and the list of everything currently on Vio.

## Disconnecting

To disconnect, open **Connection & log** and click **Disconnect from Vio**. This removes the
connection between your store and Vio — your products stop being sold on Vio.

## Troubleshooting

<Callout type="warning">
**Can't connect / key is rejected** — double-check the API key you pasted matches your Vio
dashboard exactly, with no extra spaces.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning">
**Vio API shows "Unreachable"** — open **Connection & log** and click **Re-check**. If it stays
unreachable, try again in a few minutes.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning">
**A banner asks you to add a language** — Vio delivers product feeds per country, and Shopify
requires your store to support a language for that country. Vio accepts English out of the box,
so this should be rare; if the banner appears, add one of the suggested languages under
**Settings → Languages** and it clears itself on the next visit.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning">
**Publishing from a product's page doesn't sync** — that path relies on Shopify's channel
feeds, which need your store to have a **market** for one of Vio's countries (Norway, Denmark,
Sweden or Finland) under **Settings → Markets**. Publishing from the app's **Products** screen
works regardless.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning">
**Orders don't arrive** — orders only sync back for products that are published to Vio.
Confirm the product shows **Published** on the Products screen.
</Callout>
