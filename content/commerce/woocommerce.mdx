# Connect WooCommerce to Vio

![Vio Sync for WooCommerce — seamlessly connect your WooCommerce store and keep your products, inventory and orders in sync with Vio.](/commerce/woocommerce/banner.png)

Sell your WooCommerce catalogue through Vio in a few minutes — no code required. This guide
covers installing the **Vio Sync for WooCommerce** plugin, connecting your store, syncing your
products, and receiving orders back.

<Callout type="info">
**Who this is for** — WooCommerce store owners and partners who want their products available
in Vio. You'll do everything from your WordPress admin and the Vio dashboard.
</Callout>

## Before you start

Make sure you have:

- A **WordPress site** with **WooCommerce** installed and active.
- A **Vio API key** — your Vio contact provides this for your store.
- At least one **published product** in your store (you need products to sync).

## How it works

Once you connect your store, Vio keeps your catalogue in sync and delivers your orders back:

1. Your catalogue syncs to Vio — titles, prices, stock, variants and images.
2. Your products become available in Vio's mobile apps and live-shopping channels.
3. When a shopper places an order, it's delivered straight to your WooCommerce store.

## Step 1 · Install the plugin

<Steps>

### Find the plugin

In your WordPress admin, go to **Plugins → Add New** and search for **Vio Sync for
WooCommerce**.

![The WordPress Plugins → Add New screen with "Vio Sync for WooCommerce" in the search results and the Install Now button.](/commerce/woocommerce/01-search.png)

### Install and activate

Click **Install Now**, then **Activate**. A new **Vio** menu appears in your WordPress sidebar.

![The Vio Sync for WooCommerce plugin installed, showing the Activate button.](/commerce/woocommerce/02-activate.png)

</Steps>

## Step 2 · Get your Vio API key

Vio provides your API key. Your Vio contact sends you the key for your store — you don't
need to create it yourself.

<Callout type="info">
Don't have your API key yet? [Contact the Vio team](https://vio.live) and we'll set you up.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning">
Keep your API key private — it links your store to your Vio account. Treat it like a password.
</Callout>

## Step 3 · Connect your store

<Steps>

### Open the Vio settings

In WordPress, click the **Vio** menu to open the connection screen.

### Enter your details

- Paste your **API key**.
- Choose your **environment** — **Production**, or **Staging** to test first.
- Pick your **currency**.

Then click **Connect**.

![The Vio configuration screen with the API key field, the Environment and Currency selectors, and the Connect button.](/commerce/woocommerce/04-connect.png)

### Approve the connection

WooCommerce shows an authorization screen — click **Approve** to let Vio connect to your store.

![The WooCommerce authorization screen — "Vio Sync for WooCommerce would like to connect to your store" — with the Approve button.](/commerce/woocommerce/05-authorize.png)

</Steps>

Once connected, the Vio screen shows **Connected**, with your account, a **Reachable** API
status, and **Active** order webhooks.

![The Vio configuration screen in the Connected state — connection details (account, environment, API health "Reachable", order webhooks "Active", API key "Valid") and the sync overview.](/commerce/woocommerce/06-connected.png)

## Step 4 · Sync your products

On the **Vio** screen, click **Sync all** to push your whole catalogue to Vio. You can also
sync selected products from **Products → All Products** using the **Sync** bulk action.

![The sync dialog — the progress bar full and each product marked with a green check (Done 2/2).](/commerce/woocommerce/07-sync-progress.png)

Each synced product shows a green **✓ Synced** status in the **Vio** column of your product
list.

![The WooCommerce product list with the Vio column showing a green ✓ Synced status on every row.](/commerce/woocommerce/08-synced-column.png)

<Callout type="info" emoji="🎥">
**Video** · `commerce/woocommerce/sync-demo.mp4`
<br />*Shows:* the sync flow in action — clicking **Sync all**, the progress bar, and products
turning to **✓ Synced**. ~20–30 s.
</Callout>

**What syncs:** title, description, price, stock, SKU, barcode, variants and attributes, and
image URLs. When you edit a product in WooCommerce, it re-syncs automatically on save.

## Step 5 · Receiving orders

Orders placed through Vio are delivered to your WooCommerce store automatically — they appear
in **WooCommerce → Orders** like any other order. There's no extra setup: the plugin registers
the order webhooks for you during connection.

## Managing your catalogue

From **Products → All Products**, select products and use the **Bulk actions**:

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Sync** | Push the selected products to Vio. |
| **Unsync** | Remove the link to Vio, but keep the product in your store. |
| **Delete from Vio** | Remove the product from Vio. |

The **Vio** column shows each product's status at a glance, and products you edit re-sync
automatically on save.

## Disconnecting

To disconnect, open the **Vio** menu and click **Disconnect**. This removes the API key, the
WooCommerce REST keys and the order webhooks from your store. You can optionally also delete the
products Vio had synced.

## Troubleshooting

<Callout type="warning">
**"Not connected" or the key is rejected** — double-check the API key and that you selected the
right environment (Production vs Staging). Use **Re-check** on the Vio screen.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning">
**Products don't sync** — make sure they're **Published** (drafts don't sync) and that your
store shows **Connected**. Then run **Sync all** again.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning">
**Orders don't arrive** — confirm **Order webhooks** show **Active** on the Vio screen. If they
don't, disconnect and reconnect.
</Callout>
