# Vio Commerce

Sell your store's catalogue through Vio. Connect your e-commerce platform, sync your
products, and Vio handles the rest — your products become available in Vio's mobile apps
and live-shopping channels, and orders flow straight back to your store.

## How it works

1. **Connect** your store to Vio with your API key.
2. **Sync** your catalogue — products, prices, stock, variants and images.
3. **Sell** — your products appear in Vio; shoppers buy in-stream, without leaving the experience.
4. **Receive orders** back in your store automatically.

Nothing is sent to Vio until you connect your store with your API key.
